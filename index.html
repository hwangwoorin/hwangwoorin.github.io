Woorin Hwang

Ph.D. Candidate in Educational Technology · University of Florida

Hello, I'm Woorin Hwang, a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Florida (UF). My research examines how learners develop AI literacy, interest, and informed STEM career pathways, and how (mis)conceptions about AI are associated with engagement, conceptual change, and motivation.

I'm an educational technologist who designs AI‑enhanced learning environments that trigger, maintain, and scale "interest."

Before joining the UF, I worked as an instructional designer and educator for the Educational Broadcasting System (EBS), the Korea Development Institute (KDI) School of Public Policy and Management, and several secondary schools in South Korea.

Research Interests

  • AI Literacy Interest Development Conceptual Change
  • AI-Enhanced Learning Environment Edge AI eXplainable Artificial Intelligence
  • STEM Career Development Informed Career Pathways
